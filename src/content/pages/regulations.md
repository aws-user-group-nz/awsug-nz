## Membership

- Anyone can become a member of the society by consenting in writing and
  applying to the Committee.
- A member is an individual, body corporate, or meetup.com community admitted
  under the constitution who has not ceased to be a member.
- The Committee may accept or decline an application for membership at its sole
  discretion.
- There are no subscriptions or fees for membership.

## Committee and officer roles

The Committee is the society's governing body. Officers must be members of the
society or representatives of bodies corporate / meetup communities that are
members.

Special officer roles:

- President
- Vice President
- Secretary
- Treasurer

Additional portfolio positions:

- Social Media Lead
- Sponsorship Lead
- Web Lead

## Creation of regulations

- Regulations are created by the agreement of the Committee.
- Proposals for new regulations are to be formatted as GitHub pull requests.
