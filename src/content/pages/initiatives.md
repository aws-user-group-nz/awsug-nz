The AWS User Group Aotearoa is committed to fostering a vibrant and inclusive
cloud computing community across New Zealand.

## Current initiatives

### Community engagement

We organise regular meetups, workshops and networking events across Auckland,
Wellington and Christchurch to bring together AWS enthusiasts, professionals and
learners.

### Knowledge sharing

Through our events and online platforms, we facilitate the exchange of ideas,
best practices and real-world experiences with AWS technologies.

### Professional development

We support members in their AWS certification journey and career advancement
through study groups, mentorship opportunities and technical presentations.

### AWS Community Day

Our flagship annual event brings together the broader Oceania AWS community for
a full day of learning, networking and celebrating cloud innovation.

If you have ideas for new initiatives or would like to get involved, please
reach out to our committee members or join us at one of our upcoming events.
