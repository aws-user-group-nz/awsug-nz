## Our mission

We bring together cloud professionals, developers, architects and enthusiasts to
share knowledge, learn from each other, and grow the AWS community across
Aotearoa New Zealand.

## What we do

- Host regular meetups and events across New Zealand
- Provide a platform for knowledge sharing and networking
- Connect members with AWS experts and community leaders
- Support learning and professional development
- Foster collaboration and innovation in cloud technologies

## Get involved

Whether you're just starting your cloud journey or you're a seasoned AWS
professional, there's a place for you in our community. Join our Discord server,
come along to an in-person meetup, or put your hand up to present.

Everyone is welcome. We're all in this together.

## How we're organised

- Run by a committee made up of community members, AWS Partners and AWS customers
- Registered as a charitable organisation under the "Advancement of Education" criterion
- Bi-weekly meetings to run the meetups and manage the organisation
